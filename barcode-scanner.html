Barcode Scanner

Scan food product barcodes to quickly add them to your food log. Position the barcode in the center of the camera view.

How to use the scanner

  1. Click the camera button to start scanning
  2. Allow camera access when prompted
  3. Position the barcode within the scanning frame
  4. Hold steady until the barcode is recognized
  5. Review the detected product details
